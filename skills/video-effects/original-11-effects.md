# The 11 video effects

These are the 11 effects from the reel. Each one was made in code with Claude Code, with no camera and no editing timeline.
Paste a prompt into Claude Code inside a Remotion project, then swap in your own words and colors.

## 01 Orbit
What it is: a tall phone flies up the frame while its feed scrolls at blur speed, with a small label riding on top.
Use it: the first second of a video, to stop the scroll.
Prompt: "Build a Remotion scene. A 3D phone flies up the frame and turns slowly while its feed of blurred tiles scrolls fast. A bright label rides on the phone's top edge."

## 02 Vertigo
What it is: the subject stays the same size while the camera pulls back and a corridor stretches behind it.
Use it: a shock moment, or a line you want to land hard.
Prompt: "Build a Remotion scene. A long corridor of cards lines the walls, floor and ceiling, receding to a glowing vanishing point. Keep the center card the same size while the camera dollies back."

## 03 Zoom-through
What it is: the camera crashes into a tile and bursts through it into the next layer.
Use it: moving from one idea to the next without a plain cut.
Prompt: "Build a Remotion transition. In 6 frames, crash-zoom into the center tile, pass through its surface as if it were a window, and land in a second lit grid behind it."

## 04 Headlines
What it is: a tilted 3D wall of headline cards, with columns scrolling in opposite directions.
Use it: to show that many people are talking about something.
Prompt: "Build a Remotion scene. A wall tilted back in 3D holds four columns of bold headline cards. Scroll neighboring columns in opposite directions. Headlines: [your headlines]."

## 05 Paper Cut
What it is: a warm paper sheet slaps onto a dark stage and words press onto it one at a time.
Use it: a change of texture in the middle of a dark video.
Prompt: "Build a Remotion scene. A cream paper sheet slaps down on a dark stage with a small bounce. Press these words onto it one at a time like a letterpress, in sync with the voice: [your words]."

## 06 Charts
What it is: bars grow in a staggered wave, shifting in height, color and blur from first to last.
Use it: any number or growth claim.
Prompt: "Build a Remotion scene. A dark dashboard panel where 16 bars grow from the baseline in a staggered wave. Height and color build from first bar to last, and a second wave follows."

## 07 Phone Screens
What it is: a 3D phone shows a moving feed while rings ripple out from behind it.
Use it: to show an app, a result or a product.
Prompt: "Build a Remotion scene. A 3D phone in the center shows a gently scrolling feed, with concentric rings breathing out from behind it. Small labels pop in on each side: [your labels]."

## 08 Frame Break
What it is: a character breaks out of a framed poster, which cracks into spinning tiles.
Use it: a surprise moment, or a mid-video reset.
Prompt: "Build a Remotion scene. A character inside a framed poster leans past the edge, then bursts through. The poster cracks into a 5x5 grid of tiles that spin away from the center."

## 09 Pullback
What it is: the camera starts tight on one row and rises until a whole board fills the frame.
Use it: revealing the big picture after showing the details.
Prompt: "Build a Remotion scene. Start tight on one row of a dark grid and crane up fast so row after row floods in, until the whole board of cards fills the frame."

## 10 Glitch
What it is: the picture breaks up for a few frames with color split and sliced bands, then snaps back.
Use it: a hard cut between sections, or a mistake reveal.
Prompt: "Build a Remotion transition. For 8 frames, split the red and blue channels and slice the image into horizontal bands that jump sideways, then snap back clean."

## 11 Typewriter
What it is: text types itself out in a terminal window, one character at a time, with a blinking cursor.
Use it: a closing line, or to show something being built.
Prompt: "Build a Remotion scene. A terminal window where this line types out at a natural speed with a blinking cursor, and each keystroke is timed for a click sound: [your line]."
